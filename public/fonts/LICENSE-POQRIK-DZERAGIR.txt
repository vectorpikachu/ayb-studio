Poqrik dzeragir (Փոքրիկ ձեռագիր)
================================

Author: poqrik.am (2020)
Source: https://fonter.am/en/fonts/poqrik-dzeragir

Terms (as published by the author through fonter.am):
- Free to download and free to use, personally or commercially.
- Credit is not required, but always welcome.
- You may not sell the font file itself, rename it, claim the design as
  your own, or modify it and pass the result off as the original font.

The file in this directory is an Armenian-only subset converted to WOFF2 for
web use. The letterforms are unchanged. Latin annotations use First Time
Writing (CC0), a separate hand face, through a unicode-range split.
